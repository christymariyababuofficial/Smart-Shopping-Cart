Hardware Architecture Breakdown

2D LiDAR / Ultrasonic Sensor Array

Primary Function: Target tracking & dynamic distance mapping.

  • Scans surrounding environment in real time to locate user relative to cart
  • Determines distance thresholds to maintain comfortable following gap
  • Detects blind spots around lower frame perimeter

Infrared (IR) Proximity Sensors

Primary Function: Emergency bumper protection.

  • Provides sub-millisecond interrupt signals to prevent low-height obstacle collision
  • Guards against sudden obstacles appearing directly in front or back of wheels

32-Bit Microcontroller Unit (MCU)

Primary Function: Central decision-making, signal filtering, and motor control loop.

  • Executes sensor-fusion algorithms to filter signal noise from supermarket aisles
  • Runs closed-loop PID control for smooth acceleration, steering, and braking
  • Processes serial communication from sensor arrays with low latency

Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver

Primary Function: High-current power translation for drive motors.

  • Receives PWM control signals from MCU to adjust motor velocity independently
  • Integrated thermal shutdown and current-sensing feedback for motor protection

High-Torque DC Geared Motors + Encoders

Primary Function: Differential wheel drive system.

  • High-gear ratio tuned to haul fully loaded shopping payloads up to standard store ramps
  • Optical/magnetic quadrature encoders provide real-time wheel speed feedback

Rechargeable Lithium Battery Pack

Primary Function: Uninterrupted power delivery.

  • Dual voltage rails: High-discharge rail for motors, regulated 5V/3.3V supply for logic components
  • Integrated Battery Management System (BMS) with cell balancing and charge monitoring
